About Twisted23 Parlour

Twisted23 Parlour (twisted23.com) is a people-first digital spatial mathematics and gaming pavilion engineered by Gary Francis Stout in Anchorage and Palmer, Alaska, operating in tandem with Gravity Wizards, Inc.

Our Core Mission & Thesis

Early mathematical and spatial reasoning ability strongly predicts future innovation—yet cuts to public gifted and acceleration programs mean future invention rates vary nearly 20-fold depending on family resources. Through free digital spatial gyms (Twisted23.com) and trackless bouncing FOBB flight kits (GravityWizards.com), we put everyday families first to unlock America's grassroots innovation potential.

The 3D-in-2D Orthographic Engine

Twisted23 features custom 3D-in-2D rolling dice geometry, the 24-state Top*10+Front (TF) spatial coordinate system, Base-38 time ident architecture, the 24-Tile shape matrix, Backgammon 36-Roll tournament mechanics, and CameLcase cryptography.
