About Top Hat Puzzle & Top Hat Sudoku

Created by Gary Stout and featured on FreeGamesNews (September 20, 2007), Top Hat Puzzle (“Twisted Sudoku”) is a 24-square diamond logic game — part of the Twisted23.com game world.

  • Goal: Fill every digit 1–6 once in each of the 4 Hat, 4 Diagonal, and 4 Corner 6-square patterns.
  • Two Editions: TopHatPuzzle preserves the vintage 2007 Flash look; TopHatSudoku is the modern dark-mode recreation.
  • Modes: Play Unlimited Practice freely, or enter Official Competition (ELO) on Easy & Medium (~2-minute par solves).
